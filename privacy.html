PRIVACY POLICY

Intel Collection

Anitrace is designed for tactical wildlife intelligence. To provide this service, we collect specific data points critical for field operations:

  • Precise GPS Data: We track your location in the background to provide real-time mapping, approach telemetry, and tactical SOS alerts.
  • Camera & Media: We access your camera and storage for the 5-step reporting wizard to document sightings and provide visual intelligence to other guides.
  • Phone Number: Collected during registration to verify guide identity and enable secure communication between team members.

Data Usage

Sighting data and migration patterns are processed by our AI models to improve wildlife predictions for the entire guide community. Your personal identity is only shared with verified guides within your organization or park fleet.

We do NOT sell your location history or personal intel to third-party advertisers.
