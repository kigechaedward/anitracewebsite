TERMS OF SERVICE

Rule 01: Ethical Viewing

By using Anitrace, you commit to the highest standards of conservation. You must maintain a respectful distance from all wildlife and strictly adhere to the specific regulations of the park or reserve you are operating in.

Prohibited Activities

The Anitrace platform is for professional wildlife coordination and guest experience enhancement only. The following uses are strictly prohibited and will result in immediate tactical de-authorization:

  • Using sighting intel for poaching or unauthorized hunting.
  • Deliberate reporting of false coordinates or animal counts.
  • Sharing high-priority Big Cat locations with unverified individuals.
  • Harassing wildlife to trigger specific AI prediction patterns.

Field Responsibility

Guide reputation is earned through accurate reporting. Anitrace reserves the right to suspend accounts with low confidence ratings as determined by community verification algorithms.
