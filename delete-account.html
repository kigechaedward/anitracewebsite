DELETE ACCOUNT

Warning: Irreversible Action

Deleting your Anitrace account will result in the permanent removal of your field credentials, reputation badges, and personal intelligence data.

How to Request Deletion

You may request to delete your entire account or specific subsets of data (such as activity history, images, and videos) without closing your account.

  1. Email Us: Send an email from your registered account email to support@anitrace.intel.
  2. Subject Line:
    • ● For full account deletion: ACCOUNT DELETION REQUEST - [Your Username]
    • ● For specific data deletion: DATA MODIFICATION REQUEST - [Your Username]
  3. Verification: Our field ops team will send a verification code to your registered phone number to confirm your identity before any data is purged.

Data Deletion Policy

WHATS DELETED

  • ● Full Name & Phone Number
  • ● Precise GPS History
  • ● Individual Profile Assets
  • ● Private Team Messages

WHATS RETAINED

  • ● Anonymized Sighting Data
  • ● Aggregated AI Intel
  • ● Public Leaderboard Ranks*

*Ranks are scrubbed of personal IDs but kept for historic model integrity.

Retention Period

Once verified, your account will be deactivated immediately. All personal data will be purged from our active tactical servers within 30 days.
